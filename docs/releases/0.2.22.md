# Sink 0.2.22: stable attachment identity and focused reviews

Sync correctness release. Tag: `0.2.22`, without a `v` prefix.

## Attachment identity

- Binary files now use one canonical content identity: the SHA-256 of their base64 bytes, whether stored inline, chunked, encrypted, or plaintext.
- Unchanged attachments are recognised during startup and no longer become review rows on every launch.
- Binary fast-forward and last-applied tracking use that identity instead of modification time. A timestamp-only change is not mistaken for a content change, and a same-timestamp content change is still detected.
- Missing or corrupt chunks fail closed and are retried when the chunks arrive.

## Focused reviews

- When a startup batch contains files that genuinely need a decision, only those rows appear in the review. Safe rows are applied after approval instead of expanding the modal to the whole vault.
- Physical CouchDB tombstones are excluded from conflict live-leaf checks and retirement. Application-level deletions remain live and reviewable.
- Large binary bodies are not retained for the whole review; they are reloaded and verified at apply time.

## Snapshots

- Settings shows the 5 most recent snapshots. Older snapshots remain stored on the device and are not deleted.

## Compatibility

Install all three assets together. Keep **Sync config folder** off while testing. The grey-screen, mobile, and historical-plaintext limits are unchanged.
