# 0.2.19 verification evidence

## Incident

After the 0.2.18 local rebuild, 659 legacy remote conflict branches were reviewed. Applying Local resolved most, but a six-file follow-up review repeatedly failed with:

`An unreviewed database revision arrived during conflict resolution; nothing further was retired.`

Capture showed live replication adding leaves while conflict retirement compared the reviewed leaf set. The same review also contained deleted tombstone leaves that the strict guard rejected.

## Fix

- Conflict reviews pause continuous replication before opening the decision workspace.
- Replication resumes only after apply, cancellation, denial, or snapshot failure.
- Unknown deleted leaves are ignored during retirement.
- Unknown live leaves still abort resolution without retiring anything.
